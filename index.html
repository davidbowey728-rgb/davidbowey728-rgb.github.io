01About

Molten Institute studies how intelligence emerges in large models — and how to shape it. We train and adapt language models, teach them to reason step by step, and apply them where precision matters most, including medicine and the life sciences.

I

Rigor

Every claim is measured. We design evaluations before we design models, and we report what doesn't work as carefully as what does.

II

Efficiency

Capability per unit of compute matters. We develop methods that make strong models cheaper to train, adapt and serve.

III

Application

Research is judged by what it enables — from more capable assistants to faster, better-informed drug discovery.

03Training pipeline

From data to deployment

Stage 01 / 06 Data curation
01
Data

Data curation

Text, code and scientific data are filtered for quality, deduplicated and mixed. Data quality sets the ceiling for everything that follows.

02
Foundation

Pretraining

Next-token prediction at scale builds general capability: language, world knowledge and the beginnings of reasoning.

03
Adaptation

Supervised fine-tuning

Curated demonstrations teach the model to follow instructions, use tools and respond in useful formats.

04
Alignment

Preference optimization

Pairwise preferences — through RLHF or direct methods like DPO — steer the model toward responses people actually prefer.

05
Reasoning

Reasoning training

Process supervision and reinforcement learning on verifiable problems teach the model to decompose, check and revise its work.

06
Release

Evaluation & deployment

Broad capability and safety evaluations, red-teaming and efficient serving before a model reaches users.

    Sanila

    An uncensored, frontier-class language model.

    Sanila is Molten Institute's large language model, built to compete with leading frontier models such as GPT-6, Astra and Opus 5. It combines step-based reasoning, long-context understanding and an uncensored design for direct, unfiltered answers.
