        03// The Forge
      

      

        
How a model is born

        

          Forge temperature
          300 K
        

      

    


    

      

        
01

        
        

          Phase · Data
          
Raw Ore

          
Trillions of tokens — text, code, science — filtered, deduplicated and weighted. Quality is the first law of intelligence.

        

      

      

        
02

        
        

          Phase · Pretraining
          
Smelt

          
Next-token prediction at scale melts raw text into general capability: language, knowledge and the first sparks of reasoning.

        

      

      

        
03

        
        

          Phase · Fine-tuning
          
Temper

          
Supervised and preference tuning harden the model into something precise, steerable and genuinely useful.

        

      

      

        
04

        
        

          Phase · Reasoning
          
Forge Steps

          
Process-level training teaches the model to decompose, verify, backtrack and search — thinking in steps, not leaps.

        

      

      

        
05

        
        

          Phase · Deployment
          
Quench

          
Adversarial evaluation, benchmarking and release. The model cools into its final form — and goes out into the world.
